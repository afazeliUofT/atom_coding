# H0 Manual Novelty Gate: Primary-Source Boundary

## Claim being audited

The field-defining claim is **not** that every finite stochastic channel is a mixture of deterministic channels. That fact and the nonuniqueness of such mixtures are established.

The claim that must remain genuinely new is narrower:

> Optimize over exact, succinct latent deterministic representations of a fixed channel for fully accounted certified-ML decoding work, and co-design codes through code-restricted atom fibers and likely-action confusability.

A positive computational result is not authorized for a field-defining claim until an independent reviewer confirms that this operational optimization and its code-design consequences are not already established.

## Closest primary foundations

1. C. T. Li and A. El Gamal, “Strong functional representation lemma and applications to coding theorems,” IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, 2018. Functional representation of a channel output using input-independent randomness; objective is information/coding representation, not certified ML work.
2. S. Yang, R. Xu, J. Chen, and J.-K. Zhang, “Intrinsic capacity,” IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, 2019. Nonunique convex decompositions into deterministic channels, with latent state revealed in intrinsic-capacity questions; not hidden-state search and code-fiber optimization.
3. M. Kovačević, I. Stanojević, and V. Šenk, “On the entropy of couplings,” Information and Computation, 2015; F. Cicalese, L. Gargano, and U. Vaccaro, “Minimum-entropy couplings and their applications,” IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, 2019. Optimize latent entropy/coupling, not end-to-end exact decoding work.
4. S. Compton et al., “Minimum-entropy coupling approximation guarantees beyond the majorization barrier,” AISTATS 2023. Approximation theory for coupling entropy.
5. K. R. Duffy, J. Li, and M. Médard, “Capacity-achieving guessing random additive noise decoding,” IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, 2019. Exact noise-domain ML for a fixed additive/cancellative representation.
6. Classical zero-error/confusability theory and deterministic channel decomposition must be audited for any prior representation-dependent graph optimization.
7. Functional-representation, channel simulation/synthesis, common-information, random mapping, and coupling literature must be searched for objectives equivalent to work, inverse-fiber size, or hidden-state enumeration.

## Mandatory reviewer questions

- Is “intrinsic certified ML work” equivalent to an existing intrinsic-channel, simulation, or common-information functional?
- Has any prior work optimized a deterministic-channel decomposition specifically to reduce hidden-state exact decoding complexity?
- Has code design under an optimized—not physically fixed—family of deterministic actions already been formulated?
- Are the fiber-collision factor and its exact random-code identity new, or a direct restatement of an established collision/occupancy result in this setting?
- Is the residual-mass certificate already standard branch-and-bound likelihood bounding under another name?
- Is the reversible-action pivot merely quasigroup/additive GRAND after relabeling or completion to a Latin square?
- Do any prior universal decoding or probabilistic-programming methods already perform equivalent latent-representation optimization?

## Release status

**PROVISIONAL ONLY.** The release literature search found the ingredients above but did not find the complete operational synthesis. This is not proof of novelty. An independent primary-source audit is mandatory before any positive result is called foundational or field-defining.
