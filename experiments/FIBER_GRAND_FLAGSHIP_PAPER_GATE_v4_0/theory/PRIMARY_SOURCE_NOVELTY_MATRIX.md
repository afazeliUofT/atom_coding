## Authorized narrow claim

For a fixed number of uniformly located deletions followed by survivor BSC substitutions, FIBER is an exact membership-only aggregate inverse decoder with a strict complete-tie certificate. In the arbitrary-code L0 membership-oracle model, its distinct-candidate query count is polynomially competitive, and hence exponent-optimal, when the ML best-alignment mismatch remains in the interior of the Hamming shell. Its history work additionally obeys fixed-edit Renyi moment and large-deviation upper laws.

## Prohibited claims

- a new generic fiber-guesswork random variable;
- novelty of A*, branch-and-bound, hidden-path summation, or generic candidate likelihood ordering;
- FPT or polynomial-time exact decoding for unrestricted edit count;
- processor-cycle optimality from the L0 membership-oracle converse;
- state-of-the-art system superiority without matched compiled or hardware baselines;
- real telecommunications relevance without measured post-front-end traces.
